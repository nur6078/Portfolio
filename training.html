Basic Robotics and Microcontrollers Seminar

Jahangirnagar University

A seminar on “Basic Robotics and Microcontrollers” was held at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Jahangirnagar University, on June 15, 2023.

The seminar was organized by the Jahangirnagar University Robotics Club (JURC) in collaboration with Bangladesh Open Source Network (BdOSN), with support from TechShop Bangladesh and Pi Labs Academy.

We would like to express our sincere gratitude to the organizers, participants, and everyone who attended and contributed to making this event a success. Thank you all for your enthusiastic participation and support!

AVR Series Microcontroller (Batch-L64)

TechShop Bangladesh

This session captures the students of Batch-L64 during an intensive, long-term training course on AVR Series Microcontrollers held from Sun, Jul 11 to Thu, Oct 11, 2018.

Conducted using our specialized AVR Trainer Kit Pro boards, the program uniquely integrated daily theoretical lectures, interactive reviews, and immediate practical implementation.

For university-level engineering students, such extended hands-on training is vital as it bridges the gap between theoretical classroom knowledge and industrial application. By engaging in rigorous daily prototyping, hardware interfacing, and circuit debugging, students develop critical problem-solving skills and gain the absolute confidence required to architect real-world embedded systems.

AVR Series Microcontroller (Batch-L66)
